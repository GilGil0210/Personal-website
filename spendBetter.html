SpendBetter

AI Expenses Tracker

Designed and developed SpendBetter, a full-stack personal finance web application, building the backend REST API, database architecture, and interactive frontend to deliver features such as JWT-authenticated cross-device sync, AI-powered receipt scanning, and real-time budget tracking with dynamic spending visualizations.

  • Real-time expense tracking
  • AI-powered receipt scanning
  • Personalized budgeting recommendations
  • Dynamic spending visualizations
